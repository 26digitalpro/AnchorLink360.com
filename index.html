Set It Right, Once

Anchor Details

Every anchor defaults to PSA (Portable Surface Anchor). Tap Details on any card to switch to Earth Anchor, or log Serial Number, Test Date, Minimum Required Pull, Initial Pull, and Certification Info.

Anchor details can't be changed once a location is captured — confirm everything is correct before you tap.
